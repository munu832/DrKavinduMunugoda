About Me

Research Philosophy

My research journey combines traditional ethnobotanical knowledge with cutting-edge molecular techniques to discover novel therapeutics. With over 6 years of experience, I've specialized in identifying and developing natural products from Sri Lankan biodiversity for pharmaceutical applications.

Currently at the Medical Research Institute, I'm advancing vaccine quality control protocols while pursuing antiviral therapeutics research through my ICGEB fellowship, focusing on HPV treatments derived from plant compounds.
