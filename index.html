About Me

My research journey operates at the intersection of ancient wisdom and modern computational science. I combine traditional ethnobotanical knowledge with cutting-edge molecular techniques to accelerate the hit identification process for novel therapeutics. With over 6 years of experience, I have specialized in identifying and characterizing bioactive natural products from Sri Lankan biodiversity, translating indigenous plant wisdom into rigorous pharmaceutical applications.

Current Research Focus

  • Antiviral Drug Discovery: Development of plant-based therapeutics targeting HPV episomal maintenance and viral replication machinery.
  • Computational Structural Biology: High-throughput virtual screening and blind docking of antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) using AlphaFold 3.
  • Molecular Virology: Investigating Synthetic Lethality in virus-addicted cancer cells by targeting the viral replisome.
  • Vector Control: Formulation of WHO-compliant mosquito repellents derived from underutilized aromatic plant species.
